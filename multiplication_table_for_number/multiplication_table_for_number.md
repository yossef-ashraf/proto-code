**File Name:** `multiplication_table_for_number.php`

**Task Description:**

Your goal is to return a multiplication table for a given integer from 1 to 10. Lines are separated by `\n` with no trailing newline.

**Example:**

```php
Input:  5
Output:
"1 * 5 = 5
2 * 5 = 10
3 * 5 = 15
4 * 5 = 20
5 * 5 = 25
6 * 5 = 30
7 * 5 = 35
8 * 5 = 40
9 * 5 = 45
10 * 5 = 50"
```

**Requirements:**

- Build lines for multipliers 1 through 10.
- Format each line as `"{i} * {number} = " . ($i * $number)`.
- Separate lines with `\n` without a trailing newline.
- Achieve **O(1)** time complexity and **O(1)** space complexity.
