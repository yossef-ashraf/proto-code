**File Name:** `drink_about.php`

**Task Description:**

Kids drink toddy, teens drink coke, young adults drink beer, and adults drink whisky. Make a function that receives age, and returns what people drink according to the rules:
- Children under 14 old: "drink toddy"
- Teens under 18 old: "drink coke"
- Young adults under 21 old: "drink beer"
- Adults have 21 or more: "drink whisky"

**Example:**

```php
Input:  13
Output: "drink toddy"

Input:  17
Output: "drink coke"

Input:  18
Output: "drink beer"

Input:  21
Output: "drink whisky"
```

**Requirements:**

- Check the age range using conditional statements.
- Return the exact corresponding drink message.
- Achieve **O(1)** time complexity and **O(1)** space complexity.
