**File Name:** `replace_all_vowel_to_exclamation_mark_in_the_sentence.php`

**Task Description:**

Replace all vowel to exclamation mark in the sentence. `aeiouAEIOU` are vowels.

**Example:**

```php
Input:  "Hi!"
Output: "H!!"

Input:  "!Hi! Hi!"
Output: "!H!! H!!"

Input:  "aeiou"
Output: "!!!!!"
```

**Requirements:**

- Replace all vowels (`a, e, i, o, u` case-insensitively) with `!`.
- Achieve **O(n)** time complexity.
